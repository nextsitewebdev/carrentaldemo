Before You Rent

Rental Requirements

Everything you need to know before picking up your car. Simple, transparent, no surprises.

01

What You Need

Documents & ID

Driving License

A valid driving license is required for all rentals. Foreign renters must carry an International Driving Permit (IDP) that includes a motorcycle endorsement for scooters. Thai license holders may present their Thai license directly.

Police checkpoints operate throughout Chiang Mai. Always carry your IDP when driving.

Passport or ID

All renters must present a valid passport (foreign nationals) or a Thai government-issued photo ID (Thai residents) at time of rental. A copy will be taken for our records.

Your passport must be valid for the full duration of your rental.

Minimum Age

You must be at least 18 years old to rent any vehicle from Gondal. There is no upper age limit provided you hold a valid driving license.

02

Security Deposit

Deposit & Payment

Refundable

Cash Security Deposit

A refundable cash deposit is required at the start of every rental. The exact amount depends on the vehicle type and rental duration and is confirmed when you book. Your deposit is returned in full when you return the car in its original condition.

Cash

Accepted Payment Method

Rental fees and deposits are paid in Thai Baht (THB) cash. Please ensure you have the correct amount ready at pickup. Contact us in advance if you have questions about accepted payment methods.

03

How It Works

Rental Terms

Rental Period

Rentals are available daily, weekly, or long-term. A rental day is calculated as 24 hours from pickup time. If you wish to extend, inform us in advance — the vehicle may already be reserved by another customer.

Pre-Inspection

Before driving away, a full inspection is conducted together with you. Any existing marks, scratches, or damage are documented and photographed. Please review the condition report carefully before signing.

Mileage

All rentals include unlimited mileage within Thailand. The vehicle must not be taken across international borders. Unauthorized cross-border use voids all insurance coverage.

Cancellation

Please contact us as early as possible if your plans change. Cancellation terms are discussed at time of booking. Late cancellations may incur a partial charge depending on the notice given.

04

Fuel

Fuel Policy

FULL — FULL

Our Fuel Policy

You receive the car with a full tank. Return it with a full tank. Simple.

Receive Full

The vehicle is handed to you with a full tank of fuel, verified at pickup.

Return Full

Return the vehicle with a full tank. If returned with less fuel, a refuelling fee of 150 THB applies in addition to the cost of the missing fuel.

Wrong Fuel

Always check the fuel type before filling. Diesel vehicles require diesel only. Misfuelling is the renter's full responsibility.

05

Returning the Car

Return Conditions

On Time

Return the vehicle by the agreed time. Late returns are charged at 80 THB per hour. Returns more than 3 hours late are charged a full extra day.

Clean & Tidy

Return the vehicle in a reasonably clean condition. Excessively dirty vehicles (e.g. mud-covered) incur a cleaning fee of 150 THB. Tar or paint contamination: 500 THB.

No Early Refund

Returning the vehicle before the agreed end date does not entitle you to a partial refund of prepaid rental fees.

Same Location

The vehicle must be returned to the same location it was collected from, unless a one-way arrangement was agreed in writing before your rental began.

06

Damage & Responsibility

Your Responsibility

You Are Responsible

From the moment you take possession of the vehicle until you return it, you are responsible for any damage, theft, or loss — regardless of fault — unless covered by the included insurance.

Report Immediately

Any accident, damage, or breakdown must be reported to us as soon as it occurs. Do not attempt repairs without prior authorization. Failure to report may affect your insurance coverage.

What Is Not Covered

Insurance does not cover damage caused by reckless driving, driving under the influence, unauthorized drivers, or cross-border travel. In these cases the renter is liable for the full cost of repair.

Included Insurance

Vehicle damage (non-negligent)
Driver & passenger medical cover
Third-party liability
Negligent or reckless driving
Cross-border travel
Driving under the influence
